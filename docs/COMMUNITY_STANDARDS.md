# SimRecipes community standards

SimRecipes is a photography recipe community. Public recipes and images must
be relevant to photography and must not contain pornography, sexual
exploitation, threats, harassment, hate, spam, or content that infringes
another person's rights.

Content is screened before publication where automated text checks can make a
reliable decision. Users can report a recipe, image, or profile from the app.
Reports are reviewed by an administrator and may result in content removal or
account suspension. Users can block abusive profiles; blocked profiles and
their public recipes are hidden from the blocker's feed and detail views.

For safety concerns or appeals, contact [support@simrecipes.app](mailto:support@simrecipes.app).

These controls are aligned with [Apple App Review Guideline 1.2](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#user-generated-content),
which requires filtering, reporting with timely response, blocking, and
published contact information for apps with user-generated content.
